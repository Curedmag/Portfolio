BBC’s Ros Atkins on AI, storytelling, and staying curious

By Emma Vowles | Press Gazette Interview

BBC News analysis editor Ros Atkins discusses the growing importance of data journalism and open-source intelligence in modern newsrooms.

Known for his clear and structured explainers, Atkins emphasises that clarity and curiosity are now essential skills for journalists working in fast-moving news environments.

In conversation, he reflects on how journalism has evolved as audiences increasingly expect transparency, speed, and context in equal measure.

“Data and OSINT are only going to become more important in journalism.”

Following the interview, Atkins went viral after documenting a chaotic rail journey from Penzance to London, which unexpectedly became a widely shared storytelling moment online.

The incident highlighted how modern journalists now operate across both traditional broadcast platforms and real-time social media narratives.
